# Impeccable: Aesthetic & Visual Craft

Impeccable guides the visual direction of Solonova to make sure every pixel looks like it was designed by a world-class creative studio.

## Anti-Patterns (Strictly Prohibited)

- ❌ **Generic AI Gradients**: No muddy purple-to-blue linear gradients across entire pages.
- ❌ **Default Typography**: No default system fonts, Arial, or overused plain Inter.
- ❌ **Card Nesting Soup**: Never nest cards inside cards inside cards with borders everywhere.
- ❌ **Low Contrast / Gray Mismatch**: Never use low-contrast washed-out text on colored backgrounds.
- ❌ **Bounce & Jitter**: No cartoonish bounce or elastic easing; use purposeful exponential spring curves.
- ❌ **Pure Black & White**: Never use raw `#000000` or `#ffffff`; use tinted obsidian blacks and starlight whites.

## Positive Design Laws

1. **Art Direction & Brand Aura**:
   - Palette: Deep Obsidian (`#08090C`), Cosmic Slate (`#0F1318`), Electric Mint (`#00F5D4`), Cyber Orange (`#FF5C00`), and Opal White (`#F0F4F8`).
   - Atmosphere: Luminous subtle radial glows, glassmorphic sheen (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), crisp 1px borders with subtle gradients (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).

2. **Editorial Typography**:
   - Display: `Syne` (Bold, sculptural, high-energy headline font).
   - Body & Interface: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Clean, hyper-legible, geometric humanist grotesque).
   - Code & Metrics: `Space Grotesk` or `JetBrains Mono`.

3. **Motion & Micro-interactions**:
   - Transitions: Fast in, smooth deceleration (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
   - Hover Feedback: Elevated depth, subtle glow expansion, magnetic buttons.
   - Interactive Feedback: Immediate visual confirmation on clicks, scrolls, and filter changes.
